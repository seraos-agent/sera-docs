# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how SERA OS, owned and operated by **PT SERA TEKNOLOGI DIGITAL** ("Company", "we", "our", or "us"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our autonomous AI agent services, web dashboard, and platform integrations.

## 1. Information We Collect
When you use SERA OS and connect cloud integrations, we collect and process the following information:
- **Profile Information:** Your email address, username, display name, and avatar.
- **Integration Context Data:** Metadata and content explicitly authorized by you (such as Google Drive document metadata or social platform interactions) required for the AI agent to fulfill requested automations.
- **Authentication Tokens:** Secure OAuth credentials and encrypted session tokens required to communicate securely with third-party APIs.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information exclusively to provide, maintain, and enhance the SERA OS service:
- To execute requested workflows and automate operational tasks on your behalf.
- To maintain your sovereign personal memory vault.
- We **do not** use your private data to train public foundation models.
- We **do not** sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to any third party.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
All sensitive credentials, secrets, and authentication tokens are encrypted using AES-256-GCM encryption through our zero-knowledge secret management architecture. We employ strict access controls and regular security auditing to safeguard user data.

## 4. Third-Party Integrations
Our application integrates with authorized third-party services (such as Google Workspace, Meta, and messaging platforms). Your use of those specific integrations is also subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.
